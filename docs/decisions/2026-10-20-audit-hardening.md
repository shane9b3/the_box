# Audit hardening — 2026-10-20

The owner authorized fixes from the app audit, delegated implementation/review, and emulator UI checks. Preserve the existing guide/player canvas, single SurfaceView, playback connection broker and approved Back ladder. The emulator is useful for behavior and layout; its playback performance is not a device acceptance gate.

Proposals and reconciled reviews:

- The performance reviewer proposed an explicit pending-preparation owner for time-shift, with exactly-once take/release, cleanup of late grants on IO, consumed grant metadata, and serialized counter snapshots outside the accounting lock. The security reviewer approved it after reviewing cancellation, ownership and stale-preparation races. A needed predicate skips superseded queued allocations; at most an already-running local allocation finishes and disposes. Counter flushes are asynchronous/coalesced, atomic replacement preserves file integrity, and failed snapshots remain dirty for a later flush.
- The TV UX reviewer proposed clearing Search results on access/content invalidation, checking publication epochs, containing modal focus and timing long-OK independently of native repeats. The security reviewer required current-snapshot checks around suspended actions as well. Search uses 30 design px titles and 24 design px metadata, larger cards and 96×56 design-pixel safe margins. The root fixed-canvas scale remains the baseline; larger system text requires a separate flexible-layout design rather than changing density alone.
- The lead proposed on-demand settings keys, with duplicate validation across fixed and loaded rows only. The performance reviewer approved it with sorted logical-page traversal and separate identity/index namespaces. Work is proportional to loaded rows rather than the advertised count.
- The lead proposed observing live accessibility state, providing guide activation/directional actions through the existing state machine and disabling announcements/actions outside the active grid. The TV UX reviewer approved it with the requirement that modal/PIN announcements never compete with stale programme descriptions. Preserve draw-only frame invalidation by deriving a single active-grid flag from UI state.
- Production release packaging requires valid explicit signing. Debug, benchmark and profile-generation builds remain independently buildable. CI includes app adapter tests and exports their reports.
- Usable-startup reporting occurs after the startup decision and visible destination replace the blank frame. It is distinct from first-media rendering and the eight-second blank-screen safety cap. It does not claim a startup speed improvement.
- Emulator visual review found a sync-status chip squeezing the header clock to an ellipsis. The TV UX reviewer proposed a flexible center status area and an intrinsic trailing clock, with the date yielding width before the time; the lead approved it. Deterministic text-layout tests cover 24/12-hour time, long dates and absent/short status. The obsolete sync-error copy now points to the existing Settings → TV guide section.

The linear-time user-regex engine decision is recorded separately by the security reviewer, including syntax compatibility and resource budgets.
